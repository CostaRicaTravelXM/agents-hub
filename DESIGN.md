# Design System: TravelXM Agents Hub

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Well-Marked Trail"**

An advisor, often older, opens this on a phone, an iPad or a small laptop between client calls. Like a well-marked trail, the Hub keeps the next step in plain view: every icon has a word with it, every card says what tapping does, and the way back is always on screen. Pure white ground and near-black ink carry the structure, so photographs of Costa Rica and each module's soft colour are what the eye lands on.

The density is deliberately low. Type starts at 15px, body is 17px on a phone, targets are at least 44px, and a screen has one main action in deep lime green. Rounded pills and generous 24px card corners make controls look pressable without decoration. Anton and Playfair Display survive only on certificates, where they read as a document rather than an interface.

Motion is short and quiet: one curve (`cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.6, 0.25, 1)`) and three durations, 120ms for colour, 200ms for lift and tabs, 340ms for sheets, reveals, photo zoom and the card photo morphing into the detail hero. There is no bounce. The book page turn alone runs longer (400 to 420ms on the same curve). Reduced-motion settings are respected. The system is light only.

This file records the web interface. The Supabase email templates and the downloadable PDF certificate still carry the previous palette and fonts, and are not a reference for new work.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Pure white page, ink structure, one deep green action colour
- Module identity carried by soft tints, never by solid brand colour
- One typeface across the interface
- Big targets, visible labels, nothing revealed only on hover
- Floating dark tab bar until the sidebar takes over at 1024px

## Colors

A white and ink neutral base with a single deepened green for action, and the brand's bright colours held back to tints, status and small signals.

### Primary
- **Deep Lime** (#507c13): the main action on a screen, and the fill of progress bars, checked controls and the video play button. White text on it reaches 4.96:1. Hover deepens to #456c10 and press to #3f630f.
- **Brand Lime** (#70a91b): the brand's own green, which gives only 2.85:1 under white text. In the interface it appears only as a tint: the Learning module well and the current lesson row.

### Secondary
- **Module Tints**: each section keeps a brand colour as a soft wash behind its ink icon, on Home tiles, in the More sheet and on section pages. Learning is lime at 20%, FAM Trips turquoise at 15%, Current Offers sunset at 20%, Books toucan at 30%, Marketing Assets rose at 15%, Agents Portal olive at 20%. Home, Profile and Admin use Sunken Grey. Ink on any of them stays above 13:1.

### Tertiary
- **Status Colours**: Olive (#489149) for completed marks, Toucan (#ffc300) for warnings, small award and people marks, and the video scrub thumb, Turquoise (#00b4d8) for information. On solid Toucan or Turquoise the text is black.
- **Sunset and Rose** (#f77f00, #ff477e): these exist to feed the module tints. Solid use is a rare badge tone with black text.
- **Volcanic Danger** (#b4512b): destructive actions and error text, 5.06:1 on white.

### Neutral
- **Page White** (#ffffff): the page, cards, sheets and the browser theme colour. Cards are not a different colour from the page; a hairline defines them.
- **Sunken Grey** (#f5f5f3): flat panels, placeholders, hover fills, fact pills and round close buttons.
- **Ink** (#1d1a18): headings, active navigation, the tab bar, the card arrow, the default focus ring.
- **Volcanic Stone** (#3d3733): running text (11.71:1) and the hover of ink buttons.
- **Muted** (#6b625c): secondary text and inactive icons, 5.95:1 on white and 5.45:1 on Sunken Grey.
- **Hairline** (#ebe9e6): dividers and resting card or tile borders. It never defines a control.
- **Control Grey** (#8f8b86): input and secondary button borders, 3.38:1 on white.
- **Link Green** (#2f7031): underlined text links, 6.03:1 on white, turning ink on hover.
- **Theatre Black** (#000000): only behind the lesson video.

### Named Rules
**The Deepened Lime Rule.** White text sits on Deep Lime, never on Brand Lime. Brand Lime is a tint and nothing else.

**The Ink Navigation Rule.** Where you are in the main navigation is shown in ink, not in green: an ink pill with white text in the sidebar, a white pill with ink text on the dark tab bar, an ink border on the More sheet tile. Green means "do this", so it does not also mean "you are here".

**The One Green Action Rule.** A screen has one solid green main action. Everything else uses the ink, secondary or ghost button.

**The Tint Behind Ink Rule.** A module's colour appears as a tint behind an ink icon. The tint identifies the section; it never carries text in the tint's own hue.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Be Vietnam Pro (with Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Be Vietnam Pro (with Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif)
**Label/Mono Font:** Be Vietnam Pro, weights 400, 500, 600 and 700
**Certificate Fonts:** Anton (course title, uppercase) and Playfair Display bold italic (recipient name)

**Character:** One friendly, open sans does every job, with weight and size making the hierarchy. It stays legible at 15px and warm at 64px.

### Hierarchy
The frontmatter holds phone sizes. Sizes step up at 768px. Headings use tight line heights and balanced wrapping.

| Display | 700 | 34px | 52px | 1.1 | Landing closing section |
| Title | 700 | 30px | 40px | 1.1 | Page titles, detail hero titles |
| Title small | 700 | 24px | 30px | 1.1 | Section headings |
| Card title | 600 | 20px | 20px | 1.25 | Media and course card titles |
| Subtitle | 500 | 20px | 24px | 1.2 | Supporting headings |
| Subtitle small | 600 | 18px | 20px | 1.2 | Dialog titles, empty states |
| Lead | 400 | 18px | 20px | 1.55 | The sentence under a page title |
| Body | 400 | 17px | 17px page default, 18px body role | 1.55 | Running text, inputs at 17px |

In the tokens, `--text-*` names are colours and font sizes are `--fs-*`, because the branding file declared `--text-display` and `--text-body` twice and the size silently won. Sizes below 768px are authored for this product.

### Named Rules
**The 15px Floor Rule.** No text below 15px, with one exception: the bottom tab labels drop to 14px (tracking -0.03em) under 360px wide, because five labelled tabs do not fit otherwise. Text inputs are 17px, so iOS never zooms on focus. Existing admin screens and one video-scrub time tip are still below the floor; that is debt, not a smaller tier to copy.

**The Certificates Only Rule.** Anton and Playfair Display appear on certificates and the public verification page, and nowhere else. They are not preloaded.

**The Heading Stands Alone Rule.** Headings have no small label above them; the eyebrow role does not exist. Context such as a date or a category goes below the title, in muted text, a pill or a badge.

## Layout

The app is one column with the floating bar below 1024px, which covers phones and an iPad held upright, and a 264px sticky sidebar plus content from 1024px. There is no top bar in the app shell: the page runs to the top of the screen, respecting the safe area. Content sits in a container of at most 1200px with a 16px gutter, widening to 32px from 768px.

Breakpoints are Tailwind's defaults (640, 768, 1024, 1280), plus 360px for the tab label. Spacing follows a 4px base. Home sections are separated by 40 to 56px, and page headers by 28 to 40px.

Module tiles are two columns on phones and three from 1280px, with 12 to 16px gaps. Photo cards are one column, two from 640px and three from 1280px, with 20px horizontal and 36px vertical gaps. Sideways scrolling appears only in a deliberate phone rail where the next card is visibly cut off, and the rail always has a "See all" link.

The lesson player, book reader, landing, sign-in and admin screens sit outside the app shell and have no floating bar. The lesson video fills the width on black, capped so the whole 16:9 picture fits under the 64px top bar without scrolling.

Nothing may widen the page: the page clips sideways overflow (`clip`, not `hidden`, so sticky positioning keeps working) and grid children may shrink. One overflowing element makes a phone zoom out the whole layout.

Pages reserve room at the bottom for the floating bar (108px plus the safe area). On detail pages below 1024px the main action floats above the bar on a white fade, so page text never shows between them; from 1024px the same content moves into a sticky side card.

## Elevation & Depth

Hybrid and restrained: surfaces rest flat on white, separated by hairlines. Real shadow is kept for things that float above the page. All shadows are tinted with ink or stone, never pure black.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Card edge** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #ebe9e6, 0 1px 2px rgba(29, 26, 24, 0.03)`): the default card, effectively a hairline.
- **Raised** (`box-shadow: 0 2px 6px rgba(29, 26, 24, 0.08), 0 16px 40px rgba(29, 26, 24, 0.14)`): the tab bar, dialogs, sheets, menus, toasts, tooltips, and interactive cards on hover.
- **Sticker** (`box-shadow: 0 6px 18px rgba(29, 26, 24, 0.22)`): small elements sitting directly on a photo, such as the back pill and the video scrub thumb. The same value as a drop shadow lifts the animal cut-outs.
- **Photo veil** (`box-shadow: inset 0 -80px 90px -40px rgba(43, 43, 43, 0.55)`, deepening to `inset 0 -320px 240px -120px rgba(43, 43, 43, 0.78)` on tall panels): sits under white type on a photograph.

### Named Rules
**The Float Earns Shadow Rule.** A surface gets real shadow only if it floats above the page or is responding to hover. Resting tiles use a border that darkens to ink on hover, not a shadow.

**The Veil Rule.** White type on a photograph always has a veil beneath it.

## Shapes

Soft and pressable, with a clear hierarchy of corners. Actions and navigation are full pills (999px): buttons, sidebar rows, the back link, round close buttons, badges and tags. Cards, tiles, photos, dialogs and toasts have generous 24px corners. Inputs, icon wells, menu items and lesson rows use 14px. Sheets, the detail hero's white sheet and the Home feature panel use 32px on their exposed edges. Checkboxes use 8px. The tab bar is 28px, with 24px active pills nested inside its 4px padding so the curves stay concentric.

## Components

### Buttons
Large, pill-shaped and unambiguous; every one says what it does in words.
- **Shape:** full pill. Sizes are 44px (15px text), 48px (16px, default) and 56px (17px). Icons are 20px with stroke 1.75.
- **Solid:** Deep Lime with white text, for the one main action.
- **Hover / Focus:** background shift in 120ms; press scales to 98%. Focus is a 2px ink outline at 2px offset, white on dark surfaces.
- **Secondary / Ghost / Ink / Inverse / Danger:** secondary is white with a Control Grey border that turns ink on hover; ghost is transparent with a Sunken Grey hover; ink is near-black with white text; inverse is white for dark surfaces; danger is a white pill with danger-coloured border and text. Disabled is 42% opacity. Loading adds a spinner.
- **Icon button:** a 44px circle, always with an accessible name.

### Chips
- **Badge:** at least 28px high, 15px semibold, used as a status label and on photos. On photos it is white with ink text.
- **Tag and fact pill:** 32 to 36px minimum height, 15px medium on Sunken Grey. These state facts and are not controls. The detail hero splits its meta line into one pill per fact.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 24px.
- **Background:** white, Sunken Grey for flat panels, ink for the inverse variant.
- **Shadow Strategy:** card edge at rest; raised on hover when interactive.
- **Border:** hairline when not using the card edge.
- **Internal Padding:** 20px, 24px from 768px.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** 52px high (48px for the custom select), white, Control Grey border, 14px corners, 17px text. Labels are always visible, 15px semibold, above the field. An optional field says "optional".
- **Focus:** the border and a 1px ring both turn ink; the border also darkens on hover.
- **Error / Disabled:** danger border with a 15px danger message below, announced as an alert. Disabled is 42% opacity. A failed submit keeps what the person typed.
- **Choice controls:** checked checkboxes, radios and switches fill with Deep Lime. The sign-up checkbox sits in a labelled row 48px high, so the whole row is the target.

### Navigation
- **One source:** a single list feeds the sidebar, the tab bar, the More sheet and the Home tiles, so a new module appears in all of them.
- **Bottom bar (below 1024px):** a floating ink bar, 72px high and at most 520px wide, 8 to 12px from the screen edges and 12px above the safe area. Five labelled tabs: Home, Learning, Trips, More, Profile. Each is 64px high, with a 22px icon above the word. Inactive is white at 85% (12.74:1); active is a white pill with ink text.
- **More sheet:** a bottom sheet on phones and a centred dialog from 768px, with tiles at least 76px high holding a 48px tinted icon well, a 16px name and a 15px sentence. External destinations say "Opens in a new tab" in words.
- **Sidebar (from 1024px):** 48px pill rows with 16px semibold text and 20px icons. Active is an ink pill with white text; hover is Sunken Grey. External links carry an icon and hidden "(opens in a new tab)" text. The account menu sits at the foot.

### Dialogs and Sheets
White with raised shadow over a 50% ink scrim. Three shapes: a centred dialog (24px corners, at most 560px), a sheet that rises from the bottom on phones with 32px top corners and at most 88% of the screen height, and a panel that rises on phones and slides from the right at 440px from 768px. The close button is always a visible 44px Sunken Grey circle. Toasts are white and rise above the bottom bar, with a round tone glyph and plain words.

### Media Card
The card every hub module uses. The photo comes first (16:10 by default, 24px corners, no card chrome around it), then a 20px title, one 15px line of context, and a footer that says what tapping does beside a 44px ink arrow circle. The whole card is the link. On hover the photo zooms 4% and the arrow nudges right; neither carries information. Videos add a 56px Deep Lime play circle. Course cards share this anatomy and add a progress bar or a Completed badge.

### Module Tile
One module on Home: at least 148px high (172px from 768px), white with a hairline that turns ink on hover. A 48 to 56px tinted icon well sits above the module's name (17px, 20px from 768px) and one line saying how much is inside or what it is. Folders in the marketing browser use the same construction.

### Detail Hero
The top of every detail page. An edge-to-edge photo (280px on phones, 400px from 768px, at most 42% of the screen height from 1024px) carries a white 44px back pill with the sticker shadow. A white sheet with 32px top corners rises over the photo's lower edge with the title, one pill per fact and the lead. The card's photo morphs into this hero in 340ms.

### Lesson Player
A plain white 64px top bar: a 44px round close button, the course and "Lesson 3 of 12", progress from 768px, and one ink button that names the course outline in words. Beneath it is the black theatre with the video. The current lesson row is washed in lime at 15% with a Deep Lime icon well. Video buttons are 44px with white focus rings.

### Book Reader
Lives outside the app shell, with a white top bar and 56px Previous and Next buttons. Pages can be turned by finger, button, keyboard or slider, and the slider has a 28px thumb in a 44px track.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** use Deep Lime with white text for the single main action of a screen.
- **Do** show the current place in the main navigation in ink.
- **Do** give every module a tint behind an ink icon, and add new modules through the single navigation list.
- **Do** put a visible word beside or under every icon. A bare icon button needs an accessible name and a 44px circle.
- **Do** make every target at least 44px, and let a whole card or row be the target where possible.
- **Do** end every card with words that say what tapping does.
- **Do** say "opens in a new tab" in words on tiles and buttons that leave the Hub.
- **Do** put a veil under white type on a photo, and turn the focus ring white on dark surfaces.
- **Do** show the striped placeholder when a photo is missing.
- **Do** keep the logo badge at 56px or larger, never recoloured, outlined or cropped.
- **Do** keep animal cut-outs decorative, one to three per composition, at corners and edges.
- **Do** use the one curve and the three durations, and respect reduced motion.

### Don't:
- **Don't** put white text on Brand Lime (#70a91b, 2.85:1) or use it as a solid fill.
- **Don't** set text below 15px anywhere except the bottom tab labels under 360px wide.
- **Don't** use Anton or Playfair Display outside certificates and the verification page.
- **Don't** return to the discarded look: cream page backgrounds, Anton headings, or a bright lime button with dark text.
- **Don't** use green to mark the active item in the main navigation.
- **Don't** place a small label above a heading.
- **Don't** hide information or actions behind hover, or rely on a gesture nobody was told about.
- **Don't** apply the Premium palette. It is kept in the tokens for parity with the branding file and the Hub never uses it.
- **Don't** let anything widen the page sideways; clip overflow, never hide it.
